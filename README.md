# one-line-love

Repo: https://github.com/roufnihbos/one-line-love

Bounty: "Make the Judge Fall in Love in One Line"

## The line

Voyager 1 is fifteen billion miles from home, running on less power than a fridge light, and still it phones in every day, and every day, someone on Earth picks up.

Characters: 164 (limit 240)

## Why it works

It makes a machine lovable through faithfulness instead of declaring affection. The fridge light is a small, familiar image. The last clause turns the line toward the reader: being loved means someone picks up the phone.
